About PickAI

Charting the trajectory of comprehensive intelligence.

Our Mission

We are entering an era where intelligence is no longer scarce, but abundant. PickAI was founded on the belief that this transition requires a new kind of journalism—one that is technically rigorous, fiercely independent, and deeply human.

Our mission is to cut through the noise of the "AI Hype Cycle" to provide decision-makers, researchers, and developers with the clarity they need. We track the models, the money, and the morals defining the next century of human progress.

What We Cover

  • Model Architecture: Deep dives into the transformers, SSMS, and diffusion models changing the world.
  • Policy & Regulation: Analysis of the EU AI Act, US Executive Orders, and global safety standards.
  • Tools & Utility: A curated directory of the software actually worth your time.

The Editorial Board

PickAI is maintained by a collective of former ML engineers, tech journalists, and policy researchers. We believe that to cover AI properly, you must understand the code behind the curtain.
