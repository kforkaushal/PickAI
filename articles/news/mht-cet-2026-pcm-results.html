MHT CET 2026 PCM Results: Everything You Need to Know

By PickAI Editorial Board | Jun 12, 2026

The Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test (CET) Cell is expected to release the MHT CET 2026 PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) First Attempt Results shortly. Candidates are advised to keep a close watch on the official website, cetcell.mahacet.org, as the scorecard download link is expected to be activated soon.

This highly anticipated announcement follows the successful declaration of the PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) First Attempt results on June 9, 2026. With the final answer keys for the PCM group already released, the final merit list and individual scorecards are the final steps before the Common Admission Process (CAP) begins.

Important Exam Statistics (PCB Group)

For context, the PCB results released earlier this week provide a glimpse into the scale of the 2026 examination:

Statistic Details
Total Registered 2,84,063
Total Appeared 2,63,213
Attendance Rate 92.66%
Perfect Scorers (100 Percentile) 11 Students

How to Download Your PCM Scorecard

Once the link is live, you can follow these steps to access your result:

  1. Visit the official portal: cetcell.mahacet.org.
  2. Click on the "MHT CET 2026 PCM First Attempt Result" link on the homepage.
  3. Enter your Application Number and Password (or Date of Birth).
  4. Submit the details to view your scorecard.
  5. Download and print the scorecard for future use during CAP counselling.

Details Mentioned on the Scorecard

Verify that all information on your scorecard is correct. You will find the following details:

  • Candidate's Name & Roll Number
  • Subject-wise Marks (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
  • Total & Aggregate Percentile Score
  • Qualifying Status
  • Category & Application Number

What’s Next: CAP Counselling

Following the results, the Common Admission Process (CAP) will commence. Candidates will be required to:

  • Register online for the counselling process.
  • Fill in their preferred colleges and courses based on their merit rank.
  • Participate in the seat allotment rounds.

Note: Keep your documents ready, including your Class 10th and 12th mark sheets, MHT CET scorecard, and category certificates (if applicable), as these will be required for the verification process.
