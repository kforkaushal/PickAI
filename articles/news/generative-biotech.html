AI in Protein Design: Beyond Folding

By PickAI Editorial Board | Jan 19, 2026

AlphaFold solved the reading problem. Now, new diffusion models are solving the writing problem. We are entering the era of "De Novo" protein design.

Instead of searching nature for a drug that might work, AI can now hallucinate a protein structure perfectly shaped to bind to a specific disease receptor. Early trials suggest this approach could increase the success rate of pre-clinical candidates by orders of magnitude.
