The Talent Gap: CapEx Is Ready, But Where Are The Engineers?

By PickAI Editorial Board | Jan 19, 2026

A new paradox is emerging in the Indian tech sector: While budgets for Artificial Intelligence are hitting record highs, the human capital required to execute these visions is becoming dangerously scarce.

According to recent industry surveys, 90% of Indian C-suite leaders plan to significantly increase their AI spending this year. The mandate from the top is clear: adopt, integrate, and innovate. Companies are rushing to buy the latest GPUs, license the most powerful models, and overhaul their digital infrastructure.

However, nearly 27% of these same leaders admit they are struggling to actually find enough skilled talent to run these new systems. This "Talent Gap" threatens to stall the ROI of these massive investments. Buying the software is the easy part; finding the machine learning engineers, data architects, and prompt engineers to deploy it effectively is proving to be the new battleground.

With external hiring becoming increasingly competitive and expensive, many firms are pivoting to internal upskilling. "Grow your own" talent academies are becoming as critical to business strategy as the AI models themselves. For India to maintain its fast-paced digital transformation, bridging this skills gap is no longer just an HR issue—it is a boardroom emergency.
