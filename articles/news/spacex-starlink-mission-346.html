SpaceX Starlink Mission 346: Another Reusability Record

By PickAI Editorial Board | Jan 21, 2026

SpaceX successfully launched another batch of 29 Starlink satellites from Florida this morning.

The mission, designated Starlink-346, proceeded without a hitch. The real headline, however, was the booster landing. This mission marked the 24th successful launch and landing for this specific Falcon 9 first stage.

Such extreme reusability continues to drive down the cost of access to orbit, enabling the rapid deployment of the Starlink mega-constellation which now serves millions of users worldwide.
