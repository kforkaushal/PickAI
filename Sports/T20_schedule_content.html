2026 Edition

ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026

The tenth edition of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup will be co-hosted by India and Sri Lanka from February 9 to March 8, 2026. The tournament features 20 teams competing across 56 matches in iconic venues including Eden Gardens, Wankhede Stadium, Narendra Modi Stadium, and R.Premadasa Stadium.
