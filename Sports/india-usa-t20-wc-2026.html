India Extend T20 World Cup Winning Streak to Nine

By PickAI Sports Desk | Feb 7, 2026

Mumbai – Defending champions India began their T20 World Cup 2026 campaign with a commanding 29-run victory over the USA at a packed Wankhede Stadium on Friday evening, extending their remarkable winning streak in the tournament to nine consecutive matches.

Suryakumar Yadav produced a masterclass in T20 batting, scoring an unbeaten 84 off just 49 balls to guide India to a competitive total of 161/9 after the defending champions were put into bat first by USA captain Monank Patel.

Suryakumar's Spectacular Innings

The Mumbai crowd was treated to vintage Suryakumar, who showcased his full repertoire of innovative shots. Coming to the crease at 45/3 in the 7th over, the right-hander took control of the innings with his trademark 360-degree strokeplay.

His innings included six boundaries and six towering sixes, with his strike rate of 171.43 proving crucial on a surface that offered variable bounce. The highlight was a sensational sequence in the 16th over where he dispatched three consecutive deliveries from Saurabh Netravalkar for six, four, and six.

Match Summary

India: 161/9 (20 overs)

Suryakumar Yadav 84* (49) • Hardik Pandya 24 (18) • Yashasvi Jaiswal 18 (12)

Bowling: S. Netravalkar 3/28, A. Gous 2/31, N. Kenjige 2/35

USA: 132/8 (20 overs)

M. Patel 42 (31) • A. Jones 28 (24) • C. Anderson 19 (15)

Bowling: J. Bumrah 3/18, A. Singh 2/24, R. Jadeja 2/29

India won by 29 runs

USA's Spirited Chase Falls Short

Chasing 162, the USA got off to a bright start with opener Aaron Jones looking dangerous early on. However, Jasprit Bumrah struck twice in the powerplay to put the brakes on the American innings.

Captain Monank Patel led from the front with a composed 42 off 31 deliveries, but wickets at regular intervals meant the asking rate kept climbing. Despite some lusty blows from Corey Anderson in the death overs, USA fell well short, finishing on 132/8.

Bumrah's Clinical Display

Jasprit Bumrah was exceptional with the ball, finishing with figures of 3/18 from his four overs. His ability to extract movement even under lights and maintain immaculate control made him virtually unplayable during crucial phases of the American innings.

The fast bowler's dismissal of the dangerous Aaron Jones, clean bowled with a perfectly executed yorker, was the turning point of the match.

Key Stats

  • 📊 India's 9th consecutive T20 World Cup win (tournament record)
  • 🏏 Suryakumar Yadav's 7th T20I fifty at Wankhede Stadium
  • ⚡ Bumrah's economy rate: 4.50 (best among Indian bowlers)
  • 🎯 India's powerplay score: 48/2 (8 runs per over)
  • 🔥 Suryakumar's strike rate in death overs: 225.00
  • 🌟 Player of the Match: Suryakumar Yadav

What They Said

"It's always special to play at Wankhede. The crowd here lifts you up. I just wanted to play my natural game and back my instincts. The pitch had something for the bowlers, so it was important to keep rotating strike and capitalize when they missed their lengths."

— Suryakumar Yadav, Player of the Match

"We gave a good account of ourselves. India are world-class, and we learned a lot today. Our boys showed character, and we'll take the positives into our next game. The experience will be invaluable for our young team."

— Monank Patel, USA Captain

What's Next

India's next match is against Pakistan on February 9 at Wankhede Stadium in what promises to be a blockbuster Group A encounter. The USA will look to bounce back when they face Ireland on February 10 at R. Premadasa Stadium in Colombo.

This victory means India have now won 14 of their last 15 T20I matches, cementing their status as favorites to defend their crown. The form of Suryakumar Yadav and the precision of Jasprit Bumrah will give the team management plenty of confidence as the tournament progresses.
