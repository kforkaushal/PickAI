ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026

Tournament Overview

Tenth Edition • 2026

The 2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup

The 2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup is the tenth edition of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup, co-hosted by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and Sri Lanka Cricket from 7 February to 8 March 2026.

Sri Lanka previously hosted the competition in 2012, while India hosted it in 2016. A total of twenty teams are competing in 55 matches across five venues in India and three in Sri Lanka.

India are the defending champions, having won the previous edition in 2024 by defeating South Africa in the final. Italy qualified for the Men's T20 World Cup for the first time in history.

Background & History

Tournament History

The T20 World Cup Legacy

The ICC Men's T20 World Cup is a biennial world cup for cricket in Twenty20 International (T20I) format, organised by the International Cricket Council (ICC). It was first played in 2007 in South Africa, making the 2026 tournament its tenth edition.

Previous Edition

2024 Champions: India

The ninth edition, held in 2024 in the West Indies and the United States, was contested by 20 teams. India emerged victorious after defeating South Africa in a thrilling final, claiming their second T20 World Cup title.

Tournament Format

Competition Structure

From Group Stage to Championship

The tournament follows the same format as the previous edition. The 20 qualifying teams are divided into four groups of five each.

Group Stage

Each team plays the other teams in their group once in a round-robin format. The top two teams from each group advance to the Super 8 stage, featuring a total of 40 matches.

Super 8 Stage

The eight qualified teams are divided into two groups of four. Each team plays against the others in their group once. The ICC pre-seeded eight teams based on T20I rankings, who occupy predetermined positions regardless of their group stage finish.

Knockout Stage

The top two teams from each Super 8 group advance to the semifinals, with the winners facing off in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad on 8 March 2026.

Qualification & Teams

Automatic Qualification

Hosts & Top Performers

India and Sri Lanka qualified automatically as co-hosts. The top seven teams from the 2024 tournament earned direct qualification. The remaining three spots went to the next best-ranked teams in the ICC Men's T20I Team Rankings as of 30 June 2024.

Regional Qualifiers

Pathway to the World Cup

Eight teams qualified through ICC regional qualifiers: 2 from Africa (Namibia, Zimbabwe), 2 from Europe (Italy, Netherlands), 3 from Asia-EAP (Nepal, Oman, UAE), and 1 from Americas (Canada).

Historic Achievement

Italy's First World Cup

Italy qualified for the Men's T20 World Cup for the first time in history, marking a significant milestone for European cricket. They earned their spot through the Europe qualifier in July 2025.

Notable Change

Scotland Replaces Bangladesh

Following Bangladesh's refusal to play matches in India citing security concerns, the ICC officially replaced them with Scotland, the next highest-ranked team in the ICC T20 rankings, on 24 January 2026.
